Resona is an unofficial, open source client for SoundCloud. No ads, no telemetry.

- Offline downloads without a subscription
- Lyrics for pretty much anything, even niche tracks, with a few sources chained as fallback and synced timing where it's available
- A home mix that keeps discovering new stuff from artists you already like
- Import tracks straight from Telegram chats
- A folder watcher that picks up local audio files automatically
- Favorites and playlists, with multi-select and swipe actions
- Listening stats
- Custom color themes, app icons, dynamic color that adapts to the track's artwork
- No account required, log in only if you want to sync your own likes
