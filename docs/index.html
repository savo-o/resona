auto_awesome Features

Everything you need,
nothing you don't.

Built with Jetpack Compose and Material 3 Expressive, down to the morphing artwork and the wavy seek bar.

chat_bubble

Timed comments on the bar

Comments show up at their moment in the track, with markers on the seek bar. Open the full list, jump to any moment, or leave your own at the second you are listening to.

ios_share

Lyric cards worth sharing

Pick up to four lines and get a square card in three styles: over the artwork, in the track's color, or dark with a big quote.

pick a line

palette

Customization first

Twelve artwork shapes, colors from the cover, rounded font, artwork size, dividers and more.

cloud_off

Offline and light

Save tracks for offline, cache what you play, and keep listening on a slow connection or none at all.

person_off

No account needed

Open it and press play. Sign in only for your own likes, favorites stay on your phone either way.

lyrics

Lyrics that fit

Synced lyrics from LRCLIB with per-track shift and stretch, and a Genius fallback when nothing matches.

tune

A mix that learns

Favorites, downloads, artists you like and related tracks. Skips now count against a track too.

code

Open source

Every line is on GitHub under GPL v3, and translations are made by the community.
